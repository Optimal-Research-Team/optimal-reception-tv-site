Reception TV

Start the TV

Use this on the Mac mini at the front desk. One click opens the TV picture on the screen in the waiting room, and turns this window into the remote.

Chrome blocked the TV window. Click the blocked-pop-up icon at the right of the address bar, choose Always allow, then click Start the TV again.

  1. Click Start the TV. If Chrome asks to manage windows on all your displays, choose Allow; that lets it put the picture on the TV.
  2. On the TV window, click once (or press F) to make it full screen.
  3. Keep this window open. It's the remote: pause, skip, Calm, play something now, or put a short note on screen.
