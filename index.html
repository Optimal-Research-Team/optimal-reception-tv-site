Start the TV

Use this on the Mac mini at the front desk. One click puts the picture on the waiting-room screen and turns this window into the remote.

The TV is already playing on this computer.

Chrome blocked the TV window. Click the blocked-pop-up icon at the right of the address bar, choose Always allow, then click Start the TV again.

  1. Click Start the TV. The first time, Chrome asks to manage windows on all your displays. Choose Allow so the picture lands on the TV.
  2. Click the TV picture once (or press F) to fill the screen.
  3. Keep this window open. It’s the remote: pause, skip, Calm, play something now, or put a short note on screen.

The TV plays during clinic hours. When the clinic is closed it rests on a quiet "Closed" screen, and the remote's Play anyway button brings it back. If the edges of the picture look cut off, set the TV's picture size to "Just Scan" or "Screen Fit".
